Criteria Details

Avoid Rain & Snow This ignores days where the weather forecast indicates active precipitation like rain, sleet, or snow.
Precipitation > 30% This ignores days where the chance of rain is higher than 30%, even if it is not actively raining.
Avoid High Wind This ignores days with wind speeds over 30km/h, which can blow dust and dirt onto your wet car before it dries.
Avoid Freezing This ignores days where the temperature drops below 0°C (32°F) to prevent water from freezing on your car doors and locks.
Avoid Extreme Heat This ignores days where temperatures exceed 32°C (90°F), which causes soap and water to dry too quickly and leave stubborn spots.
Avoid High UV This ignores days with a UV index higher than 7, which can bake washing chemicals directly into your paint.
Daytime Only (7 AM - 8 PM) When enabled, evaluates precipitation and wind between 7:00 AM and 8:00 PM local time. Overnight rainfall that dries by morning will not penalize daytime wash opportunities.
Rain Volume > 0.5mm Allows small trace precipitation below 0.5 mm without penalizing wash suitability.
