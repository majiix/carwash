Criteria Details

Rain & Snow: Zero Tolerance Always active. Skips any day with forecasted rain or snow accumulation, active bad weather, or precipitation chance of 30% or higher.
Avoid High Wind This skips days with wind speeds over 15km/h, which can blow dust and dirt onto your wet car before it dries.
Avoid Freezing This skips days where the temperature drops below 0°C (32°F) to prevent water from freezing on your car doors and locks.
Daytime Only (7 AM - 8 PM) When enabled, evaluates precipitation and wind between 7:00 AM and 8:00 PM local time. Overnight rainfall that dries by morning will not penalize daytime wash opportunities.
